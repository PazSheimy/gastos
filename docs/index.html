Gastos
Stays on your phone

See where your money goes, trip by trip

Gastos reads the statement file you download from your bank or card and sorts every purchase: groceries, dining, gas, flights and more. It splits personal from business, finds your trips on its own, and makes an Excel report you can keep or send to your accountant.

  1. Get your statementDownload your transactions from your bank as a CSV file.
  2. Open it hereTap Choose statement CSV. It is sorted right away.
  3. Check itFix a category, tick Business or Ask my CPA, rename a trip.
  4. DownloadGet the Excel workbook, with a sheet for each trip.

Which statement do you want to sort?

One or more files, from any of your banks. Drag them here on a computer.

    Your statement is never uploaded or saved

    The file is read by the browser on this device and nowhere else. This page has no server and no account, so there is nothing that could keep a copy. Close the tab and your statement is gone. To come back to it next month, download the transactions CSV below and open it here again.


    Keeps only your category fixes, Business and Ask my CPA ticks, notes, trips and budgets, in this browser, so next month's statement is sorted the way you like. Never the statement itself.
